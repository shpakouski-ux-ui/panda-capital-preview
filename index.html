I sent crypto on the wrong network

DepositsUpdated 21 Oct 2026 · 3 min read

If you sent a coin on a network Panda supports for that coin, but selected another one by mistake, the funds can usually be recovered.

What to do
1
Find the transaction hash

Copy it from the wallet or exchange you sent from.

2
Open a recovery request

Panda app → Profile → Help & support → Deposit recovery.

3
Wait for the review

Usually within 3 business days. A recovery fee may apply.

Funds sent on a network Panda doesn't support for this coin can't be recovered.
